# CookToFit brand kit, v1.0

**Name:** CookToFit (written as the lower-case wordmark `cooktofit`)
**Tagline:** "Every meal is a rep."
**Icon:** Monoline Flex Pan. A skillet drawn in one line, with steam rising out of it that curls into a flexed bicep.
**Palette:** Espresso & Terracotta, with full light-mode and dark-mode tokens.

Open `storyboard.html` to see the full brand storyboard.

## Type
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** 600–800 for display and headings, tracking −2% to −3%.
- **Inter** 400–600 for UI and body, with tabular figures for numbers.
- Both fonts are under the SIL Open Font Licence.

## Rules
- The dark icon is the primary mark. Keep one stroke weight. Don't fill the pan, don't recolour the bicep, and don't add shadows.
- Use terracotta for the single key action on a screen. Macro colours are for data only.
- The voice is a supportive coach who cooks. Use gym words lightly, never guilt or diet-culture language.
